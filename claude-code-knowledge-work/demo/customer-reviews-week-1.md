# Vessl — Customer Reviews, Week 1

*Demo input. The first batch of public reviews the support team has to answer. Six reviews across three channels, deliberately mixed: praise, qualified praise, and complaints — including one that support alone can't resolve.*

---

## R-101 — 5 stars — store site

"I've tried three hydration apps and quit all of them because I forgot to log anything. This just… does it. The nudge comes when I'm actually at my desk, not at 6am. Two weeks in and I've hit my goal every day."

— Priya S.

## R-102 — 4 stars — store site

"Really like the bottle and the nudges are genuinely well timed. One gripe: the app lost my history when I changed phones and there was no obvious way to get it back. Everything since has synced fine, but I lost my first month."

— Tomas L.

## R-103 — 2 stars — retailer listing

"Lid leaked into my bag on the train. Not a lot of water, but enough to soak a notebook. The bottle itself is nice and the tracking works, but I don't trust it in a bag now."

— Amara O.

## R-104 — 3 stars — app store

"Bottle: good. App: confusing. I couldn't tell whether it needed charging or whether the sensor had just stopped reporting, because the screen looks the same either way. Took me a week to work out it was the charge."

— Daniel K.

## R-105 — 5 stars — app store

"Bought it half expecting another wellness gadget to shove in a drawer. It's the opposite — I never think about it and I drink more. The progress ring is the only thing I check."

— Mei-Ling C.

## R-106 — 1 star — retailer listing

"Second unit in a month. First one stopped tracking after ten days, replacement did the same thing at about two weeks. Support were polite but I've now spent more time on this than I want to. I'd like a refund, not a third bottle."
