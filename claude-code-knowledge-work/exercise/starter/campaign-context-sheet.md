---

## Campaign at a glance

- **What it is:** Vessl's national direct-to-consumer launch. This is our first real awareness push.
- **Primary goal:** drive awareness of Vessl and first purchases during the launch window.
- **Timing:** an eight-week summer launch, when hydration is top of mind.
- **Lead channels:** creator partnerships, email, and paid social.
- **Launch offer:** early buyers get free shipping and three months of the companion app's premium features.

---

## Product recap

Vessl is a smart water bottle that tracks your hydration in real time. Sensors measure how much you drink, the app shows where you stand against your daily goal, and gentle reminders nudge you when you fall behind. The promise: hydration handled, so you can just feel good and get on with your day.

---

## Target audience

Active, health-aware people, roughly 25 to 45. They already pay attention to how they feel: they work out, they wear a watch that tracks their steps or sleep, and they like the idea of one less thing to manage. Motivated and busy, and skeptical of wellness hype. They respond to help, not lectures.

---

## Positioning and hook

- **Positioning:** Hydration, handled.
- **The hook we're leaning on:** your water bottle now keeps score, so you don't have to.

## Key selling points

1. Tracks your hydration automatically, no logging or guesswork.
2. Nudges you at the right moments so you actually hit your goal.
3. Works quietly in the background of a busy day.
4. Pairs with a simple app that shows your progress at a glance.

---

## The creator-led angle

Creators are the centerpiece of this launch. We want partners who genuinely live an active, health-aware life and can show Vessl fitting naturally into their real routine, not read a script. Authentic daily use over polished ad reads. We will need a clear point of view on the kind of creator who fits Vessl before we start outreach.

---

## What we need from this campaign work

Three connected pieces, all in the Vessl voice:
1. A **campaign messaging brief** (positioning and key messages for the launch).
2. **Launch email copy** that carries those messages to our list.
3. A **creator profile** describing the type of creator we should partner with for sponsorships.

---

## Mandatories

- Stay in the brand voice (see the brand-voice guide): encouraging, clear, a little witty, never preachy or clinical.
- Lead with how people feel, then the tech.
- No fear or guilt framing about dehydration. Motivate, don't shame.
