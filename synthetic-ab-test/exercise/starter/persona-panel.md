# Barkwell — Persona Panel (3 personas)

*Provided panel for the synthetic A/B test. Three personas built from real customer research, representing distinct owner types for the furniture-grade modern bed line. Paste one at a time into the scoring prompt.*

---

## Persona 1 — Maya, the Design-Focused Apartment Dweller

- **Age / life stage:** 31, single, urban professional.
- **Home:** a small, carefully decorated one-bedroom apartment. Everything is on display.
- **Dog:** Juniper, a 4-year-old mini poodle.
- **Cares most about:** how her home looks. She has strong taste and a tight footprint, so every object has to earn its place.
- **Goal:** a dog bed she is not embarrassed by, that reads as decor rather than pet gear.
- **Anxieties / objections:** most dog beds are eyesores she hides when guests come; she is skeptical that "designer" pet products are actually well made or just marked up.
- **Would buy if:** it genuinely looks like a piece of furniture and comes in colors that fit a modern interior.
- **Influenced by:** interior and lifestyle content, social, and how things photograph.

## Persona 2 — Rick, the Practical Multi-Dog Household

- **Age / life stage:** 44, married with kids, suburban.
- **Home:** a busy house with a yard. Function over form.
- **Dogs:** three, including a large chewer.
- **Cares most about:** durability and easy cleaning. He has thrown out too many "premium" beds that fell apart.
- **Goal:** beds that survive big dogs, chewing, and constant machine washing.
- **Anxieties / objections:** thinks fancy beds are a waste of money in his house; worried a "furniture-grade" bed will be delicate or not washable.
- **Would buy if:** it proves it is tough and washable, and the price makes sense across multiple dogs.
- **Influenced by:** reviews about durability, word of mouth, and value.

## Persona 3 — Priya, the New Puppy Owner

- **Age / life stage:** 27, first-time dog owner.
- **Home:** a rented apartment she shares with a partner.
- **Dog:** an 11-week-old puppy, still chewing and having accidents.
- **Cares most about:** not messing up an expensive purchase while the puppy is little, and easy cleanup.
- **Goal:** a safe starter choice now, with plans to invest in nicer things once the puppy is grown.
- **Anxieties / objections:** overwhelmed by options; reluctant to spend a lot on a "nice" bed a puppy might destroy.
- **Would buy if:** it feels forgiving of a puppy (easy to clean, hard to wreck) or she is reassured it is the right stage to invest.
- **Influenced by:** first-time-owner guides, other new owners, and easy return policies.
