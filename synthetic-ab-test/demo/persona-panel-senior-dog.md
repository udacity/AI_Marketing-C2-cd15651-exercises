# Demo — Senior-Dog-Owner Persona Panel (3 personas)

*Author/demo input. Panel for the worked demo, which scores three creative directions for Barkwell's **orthopedic senior-dog bed** line. Deliberately different from the exercise (the exercise uses the furniture-grade modern bed line and its own owner panel), so the demo shows the method on a fresh case rather than handing the learner the answer. The three personas map to the three angles under test: emotional/aging, clinical/vet, and practical/outcome.*

---

## Persona 1 — Eleanor, the Devoted Owner of an Aging Companion

- **Age / life stage:** 58, empty-nester, suburban homeowner.
- **Dog:** Baxter, a 12-year-old golden retriever, slowing down and greying at the muzzle.
- **Cares most about:** Baxter's comfort and dignity in his last years. The bond is deep, and she notices every sign he's getting old.
- **Goal:** a bed that feels like a kindness to an old friend, somewhere he can rest easy.
- **Anxieties / objections:** worried he's uncomfortable and can't tell her; wary of gimmicky "senior" products that trade on guilt without really helping.
- **Would buy if:** it speaks to giving an aging dog comfort and ease in his golden years, and feels genuinely caring rather than salesy.
- **Influenced by:** emotional storytelling, other owners of older dogs, and reviews that mention real comfort.

## Persona 2 — David, the Vet-Guided Pragmatist

- **Age / life stage:** 46, married, works long hours.
- **Dog:** Scout, a 9-year-old Labrador the vet just diagnosed with early arthritis and hip stiffness.
- **Cares most about:** doing what the vet recommends, backed by evidence. He wants a clinical reason to believe, not vibes.
- **Goal:** an orthopedic bed that actually supports failing joints, the kind a vet would endorse.
- **Anxieties / objections:** skeptical of "orthopedic" as a marketing label; wants proof of real support (foam density, vet backing) rather than a claim.
- **Would buy if:** the support is credible and specific, ideally vet-recommended, with substance behind the orthopedic claim.
- **Influenced by:** vet advice, clinical detail, credible sources, and specifics over adjectives.

## Persona 3 — Marcus, the Results-Only Skeptic

- **Age / life stage:** 39, single, active, city apartment.
- **Dog:** Duke, an 11-year-old boxer mix who has ignored every bed Marcus has bought and sleeps on the cold floor.
- **Cares most about:** whether the dog will actually use it. He's out of patience for beds that get avoided.
- **Goal:** a bed Duke will genuinely choose to sleep in, that visibly helps him settle and get up more easily.
- **Anxieties / objections:** has wasted money on beds his dog snubbed; distrusts both emotional and clinical pitches until he sees the dog use it.
- **Would buy if:** it's framed around the real outcome, the bed they'll actually sleep in, with evidence dogs like his take to it.
- **Influenced by:** outcome-focused reviews, before/after proof, and word of mouth from owners of stubborn dogs.
