# Demo — Score Three Creative Directions, Rank the Winner

*Instructor demo brief. Estimated running time ~10 minutes. The full worked version is in [`../solution/walkthrough.md`](../solution/walkthrough.md).*

## Setup

- **Scenario:** three ad directions for Barkwell's orthopedic senior-dog bed line, tested across three senior-dog-owner personas.
  - **A** — *"Comfort for their golden years"* (emotional/aging angle)
  - **B** — *"Vet-recommended orthopedic support"* (clinical/credibility angle)
  - **C** — *"The bed they'll actually sleep in"* (practical/outcome angle)
- **Tools:** Claude Code; the [`scoring-prompt.md`](scoring-prompt.md) and [`aggregation-prompt.md`](aggregation-prompt.md).

## What the demo demonstrates

1. Introduce the scoring dimensions (resonance, believability, relevance, distinctiveness) and the scoring prompt.
2. Run all three directions past each persona, capturing 1–5 scores per dimension.
3. Aggregate into a simple table — totals by concept and by persona.
4. Name the winner and, just as importantly, the confidence level and *why* (spread between concepts, agreement across personas).
5. State the limits out loud: small panel, synthetic reasoning over gut reaction, brief creative descriptions rather than finished ads.

## Key takeaway

A synthetic A/B test produces a ranked recommendation you can defend — but only paired with a stated confidence level and honest limits. The number is easy; the judgment is the skill.
