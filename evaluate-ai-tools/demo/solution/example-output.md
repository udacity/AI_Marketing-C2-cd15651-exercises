## Build-vs-buy check

Same copywriting task run through a general model with a goal-rich brief produced output *close* to the tool's on everyday pieces — at no marginal cost.

## Verdict

> **Pass (for now).** Strong output, but it doesn't beat the general model we already pay for by enough to justify the price and the copy-paste workflow. Revisit if copy volume grows or if brand-voice drift on the baseline becomes a real problem.

The value isn't the total — it's this documented reasoning you can hand a skeptical manager.
