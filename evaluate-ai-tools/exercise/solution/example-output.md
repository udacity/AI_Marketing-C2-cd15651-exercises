## Findings

- **Penmark** — best output, but copy-paste-only and premium-priced. The quality edge over a well-briefed general model is thin on everyday copy.
- **CopyFlow** — cheap and well-integrated, but generic output needs heavy editing; highest raw total, weakest where it counts.
- **BrandForge** — strong and well-integrated, but the standard tier may train on your inputs, and its no-training tier costs *more than Penmark*. Privacy drags it down.
- **DIY baseline** — tested across two models with a goal-rich brief: on-brand and close to BrandForge on everyday copy, at ~no marginal cost. Loses only on integration.

## Recommendation

> **Adopt the DIY baseline now — buy nothing new.** A goal-rich brief plus the general model we already pay for covers our copywriting need at no added cost or data risk. None of the three specialized tools beats that baseline by enough to justify its price and risk: Penmark's edge is too narrow, CopyFlow solves a problem we don't have, and BrandForge's privacy terms flip its price above Penmark. **Tradeoff accepted:** no native integration (manual copy-paste) at current volume. **Revisit** with a BrandForge *no-training-tier* pilot only if copy volume outgrows copy-paste.
