# Solution — Compare the Field, Write the Memo

## Scored comparison (four-way)

| Dimension (weight) | Penmark (A) | CopyFlow (B) | BrandForge (C) | DIY baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capability / brand voice **(heavy)** | 5 | 2 | 4 | 3–4* |
| Fit with stack | 2 | 5 | 5 | 2 |
| Cost vs. value | 2 | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| Data & privacy **(heavy)** | 5 | 4 | 2 | 3* |
| Learning curve / adoption | 3 | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| **Weighted read** | strong output, poor fit | cheap, weak output | good all-round, privacy risk | surprisingly close |

*\*DIY tested hands-on across two models with a goal-rich brief.*

**Dimensions I weighted heaviest:** capability/brand-voice (it's a copywriting tool — output quality is the job) and data & privacy (a training-on-your-inputs clause is a real risk for brand copy).

## The call

**Winner: the DIY baseline — "buy nothing new," for now.**

With a goal-rich brief, a general model already produces on-brand copy close to BrandForge on everyday pieces, at ~no marginal cost. None of the three specialized tools clearly *beats the baseline enough to justify its cost and risk*:

- **Penmark** — best output, but copy-paste-only and premium-priced; the quality gap over a well-briefed general model is too small to justify the price and workflow friction.
- **CopyFlow** — cheap and well-integrated, but generic output means more editing; it's solving a problem the baseline already handles.
- **BrandForge** — the closest call; strong and well-integrated, but the standard plan may train on your inputs, and the no-training tier costs *more than Penmark*. That pushes it to **pilot-only**, not adopt.

## Tradeoff accepted

The baseline loses on **stack integration** (no native CMS/scheduler — copy-paste). That's acceptable at current volume; if copy volume scales enough that copy-paste becomes the bottleneck, revisit **BrandForge (no-training tier)** as the first upgrade.

## Recommendation memo (the deliverable)

> **Recommendation:** Don't buy a new copywriting tool yet. A goal-rich brief plus the general model we already pay for covers our needs at no added cost or data risk. **Tradeoff:** we accept manual copy-paste (no integration) for now. **If volume grows** past what copy-paste can sustain, pilot BrandForge on its no-training tier — never the standard tier, given the input-training clause. **Call: adopt the baseline now; revisit in one quarter.**

## Common mistakes

- Letting the highest total score win automatically (CopyFlow) instead of weighing cost/fit/risk.
- Not scoring the **baseline**, so "buy nothing" never gets considered.
- Missing BrandForge's training-clause → price flip (the no-training tier costs more than Penmark).
