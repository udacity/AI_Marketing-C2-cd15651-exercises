# AI Tool Evaluation Rubric

*The rubric for this exercise. Score each tool on these six dimensions, 1 to 5 (1 = poor fit, 5 = excellent fit). Note the reasoning, not just the number.*

| Dimension | What you're judging |
|---|---|
| **Capability fit** | Does it do the specific job well, and do it dependably? Judge it on your real task, not the vendor demo. |
| **Brand-voice control / brand-safety** | Can you keep output on-brand and compliant? Can you lock tone, restrict vocabulary, apply brand-safety rules, or does output drift off-brand across a batch? This is the marketing-specific dimension generic checklists miss. |
| **Fit with your stack** | Does it connect to the tools you already run, or is it manual copy-paste? Does it duplicate something you already have? |
| **Cost vs. value** | Is the price justified by what it does that you couldn't otherwise do? |
| **Data & privacy** | Where does your data go? Is it used to train their models? Can you get it back out? |
| **Learning curve / adoption** | How hard is it for you and your team to actually use, roll out, and maintain? A powerful tool nobody adopts is a weak choice. |

Not every dimension carries equal weight. Weight the ones that matter most for the job in front of you, and say which and why. The scores narrow the field; your judgment makes the call.
