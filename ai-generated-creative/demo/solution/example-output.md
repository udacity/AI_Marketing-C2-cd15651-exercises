# Demo Output — Direct the Concept, Then Generate It

*The clean deliverable the demo produces. The [`walkthrough.md`](walkthrough.md) narrates how; this is just the artifact. The image itself is generated live in an image tool; this is the direction, prompt, and copy the demo hands off. Illustrative.*

## Concept

**"On track, without trying."** A real person mid-day with the Vessl bottle naturally in frame — calm, capable, hydrated. Feeling first, product second.

## Self-contained image prompt (1:1)

> Editorial lifestyle photo, bright natural daylight, airy and fresh. A 30-something person at a sunlit desk mid-task, a matte smart water bottle with a subtle LED ring beside their laptop. Cool aqua and soft-blue palette, clean white desk, one warm coral accent. Candid not posed, shallow depth of field, light condensation on the bottle. Open negative space upper-right for text. No logos, no text in image.

*(Everything the image tool needs is in the prompt — it has none of Claude Code's context.)*

## On-brand copy

- **Headline:** It keeps score, so you don't have to.
- **Tagline:** Hydration, handled.

## Format adaptation (demoed)

Same concept re-prompted for **9:16** (subject lower third, headline stacked top, more negative space) — concept and palette held constant, only the frame changes.
