# Vessl: In-Market Creative Reference Notes

*Provided input for the AI-generated creative module. Short descriptions of creative that's running in our space and how it performed, so Claude can steer toward what works and away from what doesn't. (Descriptions rather than the images themselves; the point is the pattern, not the exact asset.)*

---

## What's working (emulate the pattern, not the exact execution)

- **Candid morning-routine (competitor, paid social feed).** UGC-style, product in hand, natural window light, someone mid-routine, no studio staging. Strong save and share rate. *Takeaway: candid and real beats polished; the product lives inside a real moment.*
- **Single-benefit lifestyle (our past email hero).** One person mid-activity, one short benefit line, lots of clean space, bright. Best-performing email hero we've run (well above our CTR benchmark). *Takeaway: one idea per asset, generous whitespace, feeling over features.*

## What's underperforming (avoid)

- **Spec-forward gadget ad (competitor).** Dark, techy background, feature callouts and stats layered on the product, hero shot on a pedestal. Low engagement, high scroll-past. *Takeaway: feature-listing and a "device" look repel this audience; it reads cold and skippable.*
- **Guilt-hook static (category).** "Are you drinking enough?" as the headline over a stat. Poor engagement, some negative comments. *Takeaway: fear/guilt framing backfires here.*

## The pattern to hold to
Candid over glossy. One idea over a feature list. Bright and warm over dark and techy. Feeling over specs. Never scold.
