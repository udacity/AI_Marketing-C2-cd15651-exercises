# Vessl — Launch Creative Brief

*Provided input for the AI-generated marketing creative module. Use this brief, plus the brand-voice guide, to direct the concept and generate the creative set.*

---

## Background

Vessl is a smart water bottle that tracks your hydration in real time and nudges you when you fall behind. We are running our first national launch this summer. We need social and ad creative that introduces the brand and makes people feel the payoff of effortless hydration.

## Objective

Stop the scroll and land one clear idea: Vessl keeps score, so you don't have to. Drive awareness and clicks to the launch page.

## Audience

Active, health-aware people, 25 to 45. They already track their steps or sleep and like the idea of one less thing to manage. They are drawn to feeling good, not to gadgets or hype.

## Single key message

Your water bottle now handles your hydration for you, so you can just feel your best and get on with your day.

## Tone

Bright, encouraging, calm, and a little witty. Human over techy. Show the feeling first, the product second. Never clinical, never preachy, never fear-based.

## Deliverables

One campaign concept, produced as a variant set across three aspect ratios:
- **1:1** for the social feed
- **9:16** for stories and reels
- **16:9** for a display banner

Each variant should share the same concept, brand look, and core message, adapted to the frame.

## Mandatories

- Follow the look-and-feel in the brand-voice guide: cool aquas and soft blues, a clean white base, one warm accent, bright daylight, candid real-life moments, the bottle as the hero.
- Include the tagline: **Hydration, handled.**
- Keep any on-image copy short and in the brand voice.

## What success looks like

Someone in the target audience sees it, instantly gets what Vessl does, and feels like it would make their day a little easier. It looks like a real brand, not a stock ad.
