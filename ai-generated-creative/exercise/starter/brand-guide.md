# Vessl: Brand Guide

*Provided asset for this module. The brand's source of truth for voice, color, and typography. Not an exhaustive logo manual; it covers what you need to direct on-brand creative.*

---

## Brand snapshot

**What Vessl is:** a smart water bottle that tracks your hydration in real time. Sensors in the bottle measure how much you drink, the companion app shows where you stand against your daily goal, and gentle reminders nudge you when you fall behind.

**What we believe:** staying hydrated shouldn't take willpower or math. Vessl handles the tracking and the nudging so you can just feel good and get on with your day.

**One-line positioning:** Hydration, handled.

---

## Who we talk to

Active, health-aware people, roughly 25 to 45, who already pay attention to how they feel: they work out, they wear a watch that counts steps or tracks sleep, and they like the idea of one less thing to think about. Motivated, busy, and a little skeptical of wellness hype. They want help, not a lecture.

---

## Personality

Vessl sounds like a supportive friend who happens to be organized, not a coach, a doctor, or a guru.

- **Encouraging**, never nagging.
- **Clear**, never clinical.
- **Upbeat**, with a light sense of humor, never goofy.
- **Confident**, never boastful.

---

## Voice and tone

- Talk to the reader directly, as "you."
- Keep sentences short and plain. If a line sounds like a spec sheet, rewrite it.
- Be warm and motivating. Celebrate small wins ("nice, you're ahead of schedule") instead of flagging failures.
- Lead with how the person feels, then mention the tech. The feeling is the product; the sensors are how we deliver it.
- A little wit is welcome. A pun about water is fine in moderation.

**Words we avoid:** fear or guilt ("dangerously dehydrated," "you're behind"), clinical or spec-sheet language, wellness mysticism, and hype ("revolutionary," "game-changing").

---

## Color palette

Cool aquas and soft blues on a clean white base, with one warm accent for energy and calls to action. Use plenty of white space; the palette should feel hydrated and calm, never dark or techy.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Vessl Aqua | `#16B1C7` | The core brand color. Bottle accents, key UI, primary brand moments. |
| Secondary | Sky | `#8FD2E3` | Soft supporting blue for backgrounds, gradients, and fills. |
| Deep | Marine | `#12414E` | Dark teal for headlines and text on light backgrounds. Our near-black. |
| Base | White | `#FFFFFF` | The dominant background. Keep it clean and open. |
| Base tint | Mist | `#F2FAFC` | Off-white for subtle sections and cards, so white-on-white still reads. |
| Accent | Coral | `#FF7A59` | Warm accent for CTAs and energy. Use sparingly, one accent per composition. |
| Neutral | Slate | `#5B6B70` | Secondary text and captions. |

**Rules of thumb:** white or Mist dominates, aqua and sky carry the brand, Marine is for text/contrast, and Coral is the single warm pop, used only where you want the eye to go (the CTA). Don't introduce colors outside this set.

---

## Typography

A modern, friendly sans-serif system. Clean and readable, never futuristic or techy.

- **Headlines / display:** **Poppins** (SemiBold, 600). Geometric and warm; use for headlines and the tagline.
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** (Regular 400, Medium 500 for emphasis). Highly legible at small sizes.
- **Fallback stack:** if Poppins or Inter aren't available, a clean geometric sans (e.g., system-ui / Helvetica Neue / Arial). Never a serif, a slab, or a condensed/techy face.
- **Setting:** generous line spacing and left-aligned by default. Headlines can go tight and large; keep body relaxed and airy.

---

## Look and feel (imagery)

- **Overall mood:** clean, bright, fresh. Open space and natural daylight. Hydrated and calm, never cluttered or clinical.
- **Imagery:** real people mid-day and mid-motion, a walk, a desk, a gym bag, with the bottle naturally in frame. Candid over posed. Daylight over studio. Small hydration cues welcome (condensation, a bead of water).
- **The bottle is the hero.** When in doubt, show the product in a real moment of someone's day.

---

## Tagline and message starters

- **Tagline:** Hydration, handled.
- **Message starters:** "One less thing to think about." / "It keeps score, so you don't have to." / "Feel your best, without the effort."
