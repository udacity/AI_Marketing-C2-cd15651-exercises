# Vessl: Campaign Aesthetic Addendum: "In Your Corner"

*Provided input for the AI-generated creative exercise. This sits ON TOP of the core brand guide. The brand guide still governs; this addendum only says how the brand flexes for this one intense, boxing and MMA campaign. When in doubt, the brand guide wins.*

---

## Why an addendum

Vessl's core look is bright, calm, and daylight-clean, that's right for the general brand, but it would read as soft in a boxing gym. This campaign needs intensity and grit while staying unmistakably Vessl. The addendum defines what flexes and what does not, so the flyer looks like a fighter's brand without becoming a different brand.

## What flexes for this campaign

- **Palette goes darker and higher-contrast.** Lead with deep grounds, Marine `#12414E` and near-black, instead of the white base. Use **Vessl Aqua `#16B1C7` as a charged accent**: edge light, a glow on the bottle, a rim of color in the dark. **Coral `#FF7A59`** stays the single warm pop, reserved for the CTA.
- **Light gets dramatic and directional.** Hard gym light, deep shadows, sweat highlights, a spotlit subject against a dark room. The opposite of the core brand's soft daylight.
- **Imagery is gritty and candid.** Real athletes mid-training: wraps, gloves, a heavy bag, focus and effort, sweat. Motion and intensity over posed hero shots. The bottle is part of the training kit (on the ring apron, by the wraps), present, not on a pedestal.
- **Type can go bolder.** Same families (Poppins headlines, Inter body), but headlines can run tighter, larger, and uppercase for impact.
- **Mood:** focused, earned, high-energy.

## What stays sacred (does not flex)

- **The tagline "Hydration, handled."**, unchanged.
- **Aqua identifies the brand.** However dark the frame, Vessl Aqua has to be present and recognizable somewhere; it's what keeps this as Vessl.
- **No fear or guilt framing.** Intensity is fine; shaming the athlete is not. No "you're falling behind."
- **Feeling and performance over spec-sheet.** Still no biometric jargon or feature-listing.
- **Real moment, not a staged gadget shot.** Grit, yes; fake, no.

## The line to walk
Darker, harder, more intense than the core brand, but a fighter should still recognize the aqua, feel the calm confidence underneath, and never feel scolded. Intense Vessl, not a different company.
