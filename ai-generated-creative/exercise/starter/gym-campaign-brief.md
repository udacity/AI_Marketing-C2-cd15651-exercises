# Vessl: "In Your Corner" Campaign Brief

*Provided input for the AI-generated creative exercise. A specific campaign, distinct from Vessl's general launch. Use this with the brand guide and the campaign aesthetic addendum.*

---

## Background

Vessl is a smart water bottle that tracks hydration and nudges you when you fall behind. We've launched to a general wellness audience. Now we're breaking into a new, harder-edged market: **boxing and MMA gyms.** These athletes cut weight, rehydrate on a schedule, and treat hydration as performance, not self-care. If Vessl earns credibility here, it earns it everywhere.

## Objective

Introduce Vessl to serious boxing and MMA athletes and get them to a launch offer. Land one idea: **hydration is part of your training, and Vessl handles it so you can focus on the work.** Drive a scan/visit to the launch page.

## Channel and format

A **printed direct-mail flyer** sent to boxing and MMA gyms across the country, to be posted on gym boards and handed out at the front desk. Design for print: one strong hero visual, short high-impact copy, the tagline, and a clear CTA with a QR code to the offer. It has to stop someone in a loud, busy gym, not a scrolling feed.

## Audience

Serious, competitive boxing and MMA athletes who train hard and often, roughly 18 to 40. They sweat a lot and already think about recovery and performance. They respect earned confidence and discipline, and they have zero patience for wellness fluff, gimmicks, or anything that looks soft.

## Single key message

Hydration is part of the work, and Vessl handles it, so you can put everything into training.

## Tone

Focused, intense, and confident. Earned, not hyped. Gritty but not grim. This is the performance edge of the brand, not the spa version. Still never fear-based or shaming, respect the athlete.

## Deliverables

One direct-mail flyer concept for boxing and MMA gyms, produced and then iterated at least once to improve it.

## Mandatories

- Follow the **campaign aesthetic addendum** for how the Vessl brand flexes for this campaign (it stays recognizably Vessl while going more intense).
- Include the tagline: **Hydration, handled.**
- Include a clear CTA and a placeholder QR code / short URL to the launch offer.
- Keep on-image copy short and high-impact; this is a flyer, not a paragraph.

## What success looks like

A fighter walking past the gym board stops, gets what Vessl does in a second, and reads it as built for someone like them, not borrowed from a yoga ad.
