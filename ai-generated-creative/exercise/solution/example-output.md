# Sample Output: Take the Brand Into a New Market

*Illustrative Claude output for the gym-flyer exercise, using the brand guide + gym campaign brief + campaign aesthetic addendum. Direction-only: the flyer is generated live in an external image tool and is not included here. Copy stays placeholder; the exercise aligns on visual direction. One run; outputs vary.*

## Concept directions (ideas, not generated creatives)

Claude proposes several flyer directions that hit the intense campaign aesthetic while holding the non-negotiables from the addendum (aqua present as a glow, Coral reserved for the CTA, no fear/guilt, bottle as kit not pedestal, tagline locked). A strong, testable pair:

> **Concept A, Mid-Round (kinetic).** Fighter mid-strike or working the bag, motion blur, frozen sweat, near-monochrome high contrast, Vessl Aqua isolated as the one surviving color on the bottle. Loudest, poster energy. Tests whether raw stopping-power drives the scan.
>
> **Concept B, The Corner (recovery/preparation).** Fighter on the stool between rounds, single hard spotlight against near-black, aqua as a rim-light on the bottle. Quieter, earned confidence. Tests whether the "part of being a serious athlete" moment earns more trust.

Both feature the athlete at comparable production value, so a winner tells you *action vs. stillness*, a clean A/B rather than "person vs. no person."

## Self-contained image prompt (chosen concept, print flyer)

> Gritty editorial photo for a print flyer, dramatic directional gym lighting, deep shadows. [Mid-round action OR between-rounds on the stool], real boxing/MMA athlete, sweat catching the light, a matte smart water bottle present as part of the training kit (on the ring apron / by the wraps). Palette: deep Marine `#12414E` and near-black ground, Vessl Aqua `#16B1C7` as a charged rim-light/glow on the bottle so it stays identifying, one Coral `#FF7A59` accent reserved for the CTA area only. High contrast, near-monochrome except the aqua. Reserve a clear dark bar along the bottom third for the tagline and CTA, and open space for a headline. No fear/guilt tone. No logos, no final text in image (placeholder blocks only).

Type is composited afterward (`no final text in image`), because image tools render type unreliably; the tagline and placeholder copy sit in the reserved space.

## Iteration note (illustrative)

First generation came back so dark the aqua disappeared, breaking the "aqua must identify the brand" rule, and the coral had leaked onto a glove. Re-prompting to force an aqua rim-light on the bottle and confine coral to the CTA bar produced an on-addendum result on the second pass: intense and gym-native, but still unmistakably Vessl.

## Decision memo

Close with a short memo: the visual direction chosen, why it fits the brief and the aesthetic addendum while staying on brand, the alternatives set aside, and the one improvement the iteration made, noting copy is still to be written.
