# Demo — Turn Claude Into an Adversarial Reviewer

*Instructor demo brief. Estimated running time ~8 minutes. The full worked version is in [`../solution/walkthrough.md`](../solution/walkthrough.md).*

## Setup

- **Product:** a freemium productivity SaaS with a confident conversion strategy.
- **Tool:** Claude Code.
- **Input:** no dataset. The demo works from one strategy stated on screen (below).

**Strategy to put on screen:**
> "Offer every free user their first month of Premium free — let them live in the paid tier for a month and they'll be hooked enough to keep paying."

## What the demo demonstrates

1. Ask Claude the naive way — *"Is this a good idea?"* — and note how agreeable and useless the answer is.
2. Reframe with an adversarial-reviewer prompt: assign Claude the role of a skeptical growth lead whose job is to find the 3–5 assumptions the strategy would fail on and name the strongest counter-case.
3. Read the surfaced assumptions and pick the most dangerous one.
4. Model judgment on the critique itself — name one thing the reviewer overstated and explain why you'd discount it.
5. Push once more: ask how you'd *test* the most dangerous assumption cheaply before committing.
6. Contrast a pressure-tested strategy with one that's merely been agreed with.

## Key takeaway

AI's default mode is agreement. The value is in the adversarial reframe — you use it to attack your own thinking before the market does.
