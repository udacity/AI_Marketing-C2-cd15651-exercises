# Sample Output — Stress-Test a Strategy

*A clean example of the deliverable a learner submits. One plausible version.*

**Strategy:** move cross-device sync behind the Premium paywall.

**Naive evaluation (saved):** Claude broadly agrees — sync is sticky, competitors charge for it. No assumptions surfaced.

**Ranked hidden assumptions (most → least dangerous):**
1. Users *convert* rather than churn/resent when a free daily feature is removed.
2. Sync isn't easily replaced (competitor free tier, manual workarounds).
3. The free tier stays attractive enough to keep signups flowing.
4. The support/brand cost is tolerable.

**Critique I discounted:** "this will destroy the brand permanently" — overstated for a reversible, well-communicated pricing change.

**Cheap test for the top risk:** gate sync for a small random slice of *new* signups only; watch 30–60 day conversion *and* churn/complaints vs. a control before any broad rollout.

**Revised position:** *Pilot on new signups only. Deciding reason: the play rests entirely on "convert, not churn," which is untested. Changes my call: a clean conversion lift with no churn spike in the pilot.*
