# Strategy Brief — Flowline Free-to-Paid Conversion Play

*Provided input for the "Use AI to Stress-Test a Marketing Strategy" exercise. This is an internal strategy brief. Your job is to run it through an adversarial review, surface its hidden assumptions, and decide what you would change.*

**Product:** Flowline is a freemium productivity app for managing projects and tasks. The free tier is generous — it includes cross-device sync. Premium adds unlimited projects and workflow shortcuts.

---

## The play

Move cross-device sync, which is currently free and used daily by most of our active free users, behind the Premium paywall.

## The thinking

Cross-device sync is one of the most-used features in the free tier. People rely on it to move between their phone and their laptop throughout the day. If we make it a Premium-only feature, the users who depend on it most will have a strong, daily reason to upgrade. It is the feature they would miss the moment it is gone.

## Why we believe this will work

- Sync is a habit. Once people build their workflow around moving between devices, going without it is painful.
- Our most engaged free users already get a lot of value from Flowline. This gives them a concrete reason to finally pay.
- Competitors charge for sync too, so it is a defensible place to draw the paid line.

## Projected outcome

We expect a meaningful lift in free-to-paid conversion, concentrated among our most active free users, the exact group most likely to convert. We would roll it out to new signups first, then existing free users.

## Recommendation

Proceed. This is a high-intent, low-cost change that turns our most-used free feature into our clearest reason to upgrade.
