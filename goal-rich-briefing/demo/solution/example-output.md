# Demo Output — Anatomy of a Goal-Rich Brief

*The clean deliverable the demo produces. The [`walkthrough.md`](walkthrough.md) narrates how; this is just the artifact. Illustrative.*

## Before — naked prompt

> "Write an email to get free users to upgrade to paid."

**Output (abridged):** a generic "Upgrade today and unlock powerful features!" email — no specific audience, no real reason, interchangeable with any product.

## The brief

- **Audience:** free users active in the last 30 days who have never hit the paywall.
- **Goal:** earn a click to the pricing page.
- **Context:** they already get value from the free tier; this is a value message, not a first-touch intro.
- **Constraints:** no discounting; short; one CTA.
- **Success criteria:** click to pricing (not just an open).

## After — goal-rich output

> **Subject:** You've been getting a lot done in Flowline
>
> You've been active this month — nice. If you've started bumping into the ceiling of the free plan (projects, cross-device sync, the shortcuts that make it faster), Premium is built for exactly where you are now.
>
> No pressure and no discount gimmicks — just take a look at what Premium adds and decide if it's worth it for how you work.
>
> **[See what Premium adds →]**

## What changed

Sharper CTA (see pricing, not "upgrade now"), a real moment ("you've been active — you're hitting the ceiling"), and it speaks to someone who already knows the product instead of re-selling it.
