# Demo Output — Anatomy of a Goal-Rich Brief

*Actual Claude output — produced by running the prompts below. One run; generative outputs vary run to run.*

## Run 1 — the naked prompt

**Prompt:** `Write an email to get free users to upgrade to paid.`

**Claude's output:**
> **Subject:** Unlock the Full Power of Your Account
>
> Hi there,
>
> You've been enjoying everything our free plan has to offer — but did you know there's so much more waiting for you? Upgrade to Premium today and unlock unlimited access, powerful advanced features, and a faster, smoother experience.
>
> Thousands of users have already made the switch and never looked back. Don't miss out — take your productivity to the next level.
>
> **Upgrade to Premium today!**
>
> Best,
> The Team

*Generic and interchangeable — no specific audience, no real reason, could be any product.*

## Run 2 — the goal-rich brief

**Brief:** Audience = free users active in the last 30 days who've never hit the paywall · Goal = click through to the pricing page · Context = they already get value from the free tier (value message, not a promo) · Constraints = no discount, short, one CTA · Success = a click to pricing.

**Prompt:** the brief above + "Write the re-engagement email."

**Claude's output:**
> **Subject:** You've been busy in Flowline lately
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Flowline's clearly become part of how you work. If you've started bumping into the edges of the free plan (a cap on projects, no sync between your phone and laptop, the shortcuts that trim the repetitive stuff), that's exactly what Premium opens up.
>
> No discount, no countdown — just take a look at what's included and decide whether it fits how you work.
>
> **See what's in Premium →**
>
> — The Flowline Team

## What changed

Sharper CTA (*see pricing*, not *upgrade now*), a concrete moment (hitting the ceiling), and it speaks to someone who already knows the product instead of re-pitching it.
