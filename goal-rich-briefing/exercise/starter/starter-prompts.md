# Goal-Rich Brief Exercise: Starter Prompts

*Provided input for the "One Goal, Four Prompts" exercise.*

## The product

Flowline is a freemium productivity app where people manage their projects and tasks. The free tier is generous. **Premium** adds cross-device sync, unlimited projects, and workflow shortcuts that make the app noticeably faster to use.

A few facts you'll want for a good brief:

- Premium is **$96/year** billed annually, which works out to **$8/month versus $12 billed monthly**, so annual saves about **33%**.
- Flowline **does not run discounts** on Premium (price integrity).
- Premium is **cancel-anytime**, with a **14-day money-back guarantee**.
- The most common reason people abandon the **annual** checkout is hesitation at paying a full year up front, even though it's the cheaper option.

You run lifecycle and conversion messaging.

## The task (the same for all four)

Write an **abandoned-cart email** to a user who reached checkout for **annual Premium** and left without completing the purchase.

## The three weak prompts

Below are three deliberately weak prompts for that same email, each broken in a different way. Run each one, diagnose how it fails, then write your own goal-rich brief for the same email and compare all four outputs.

**Prompt 1 (over-prescribed):**
> "Write an abandoned-cart email. Exactly four sentences. Open with a question. Mention our 20-percent-faster workflow. Add a countdown timer. End with a P.S."

**Prompt 2 (under-specified):**
> "Write an abandoned-cart email for Flowline."

**Prompt 3 (vague):**
> "Some people didn't finish upgrading to Premium. Write something to reconnect with them."
