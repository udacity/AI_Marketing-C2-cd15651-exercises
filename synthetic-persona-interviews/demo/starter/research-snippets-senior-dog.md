# Barkwell — Research Snippets (Senior-Dog Owner)

*Research for the demo. Real-sounding (but fictional) product reviews, survey quotes, and support-ticket themes for an owner of a senior dog. Use them to seed a persona grounded in evidence, not invention.*

**About Barkwell:** Barkwell makes durable, practical dog beds, known for holding up to chewing, big dogs, and endless washing. The company is considering an orthopedic line for aging dogs.

---

## Owner of a senior dog (orthopedic angle)

**Product reviews**
- "My 12-year-old lab struggles to get up in the morning. This bed is the first one she'll actually settle into. Wish the padding were even thicker for older joints."
- "Bought this after our vet mentioned arthritis. He sleeps through the night now instead of shifting around. I'd have paid double for that."
- "Good bed, but for a senior dog I really want more support. The foam flattens after a few months and then it's back to the hard floor for him."

**Survey quotes**
- "As my dog's gotten older, comfort is the only thing I care about. Looks don't matter. I just want her to not be in pain."
- "I check on him at night. If he's restless, I feel terrible. I'd spend whatever it takes to help him rest."

**Support-ticket themes**
- Repeated questions asking whether beds are "orthopedic" or "good for arthritis / hip problems."
- Requests for firmer or thicker foam options for large, older dogs.
