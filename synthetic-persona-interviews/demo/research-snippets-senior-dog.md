# Barkwell Customer Research

Barkwell makes durable, practical dog beds. To understand one group of our customers better, owners of senior dogs, we gathered recent product reviews, survey responses, and support tickets.

## Product reviews

- "My 12-year-old lab struggles to get up in the morning. This is the first bed she'll actually settle into. I just wish the padding were thicker for her older joints."
- "Bought this after our vet mentioned arthritis in our shepherd. He sleeps through the night now instead of shifting around all evening. I'd have paid double for that."
- "Good bed, but for an older dog I really want more support. The foam flattened after a few months and my big guy was back on the hard floor."

## Survey responses

- "As my dog has gotten older, comfort is the only thing I care about. Looks don't matter to me at all. I just want her not to be in pain."
- "I check on him during the night. If he seems restless, I feel terrible. I'd spend whatever it takes to help him rest."
- "We've tried three different beds this year. Our girl is thirteen and nothing seems soft enough for her hips anymore."

## Support tickets (common themes)

- Repeated questions about whether a bed is "orthopedic" or "good for arthritis and hip problems."
- Requests for firmer or thicker foam options for large, older dogs.
- Owners asking which bed is best for a dog that has trouble getting up.
