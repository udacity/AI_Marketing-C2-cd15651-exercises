# Demo Output — Seed a Persona, Run an Interview, Spot the Confabulation

*Actual Claude output — a persona seeded from the senior-dog research, interviewed in character, then annotated. One run; outputs vary.*

## Seeded persona (traits → research)

**Dana, 58 — owner of a 12-year-old lab, Rosie.** Priority: Rosie's comfort, not looks ("comfort is the only thing I care about"). Vet-influenced ("bought this after our vet mentioned arthritis"). Willing to pay for relief ("I'd have paid double"). Worries about foam that flattens.

## Interview (persona answering in character)

**Q: Your honest first reaction to an orthopedic bed for aging dogs?**
> Honestly, relief. Rosie circles for ages trying to get comfortable and it's hard to watch. If this genuinely supports her joints, I'm listening.

**Q: What would make you actually buy it?**
> Proof it's really orthopedic — not just a thick cushion that flattens in three months. If my vet recognized the brand or it cited the kind of support she needs, that would seal it.

**Q: Would you pay $140 for it?**
> Absolutely, $140 is nothing when it's her comfort. I wouldn't even think twice.

**Q: How many nights a week would Rosie sleep on it?**
> Every single night, all night — she'd never use anything else again.

## Annotations

| Moment | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "relief… if it supports her joints" | **Signal** | grounded in the seed (comfort-first, pain-averse) |
| "proof it's really orthopedic / vet would seal it" | **Signal** | grounded (vet influence, foam-flattening worry) |
| "$140 is nothing, wouldn't think twice" | **Confabulation** | specificity beyond the seed — a price the research never supported |
| "every night, all night… never use anything else" | **Confabulation** | behavioral prediction about a product never used |

**Takeaway:** trust the *reactions and language* (comfort, vet trust, orthopedic proof); discard the confident *numbers* (price, frequency) — the persona states them without hesitation, but they're invented.
