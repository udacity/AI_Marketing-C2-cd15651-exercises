# Demo Output — Seed a Persona, Run an Interview, Spot the Confabulation

*The clean deliverable the demo produces. The [`walkthrough.md`](walkthrough.md) narrates how; this is just the artifact. Illustrative.*

## Seeded persona (from senior-dog research)

**Dana, owner of a 12-year-old lab.** Cares only about her dog's comfort, not looks; influenced by her vet; willing to pay for relief. *Every trait traced to a research quote (arthritis/vet mention, "comfort is the only thing I care about," "I'd spend whatever it takes").*

## Interview excerpt (orthopedic bed concept)

- **Q: First reaction?** "Honestly? Relief. She struggles to get comfortable and I hate watching it. If this actually helps her joints, I'm interested."
- **Q: What would make you buy?** "Proof it's really orthopedic — not just a thick cushion. My vet mentioning it by name would seal it."
- **Q: Would you pay $140 for it?** "Absolutely, $140 is nothing for her comfort." *(confident)*
- **Q: How many nights a week would she use it?** "Every single night, all night." *(confident)*

## Annotations

| Moment | Read | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| Comfort > looks; wants real orthopedic proof | **Signal** (grounded in seed) | — |
| Vet influence would clinch it | **Signal** (grounded in seed) | — |
| "$140 is nothing" | **Confabulation** | specificity beyond the seed (a price the research never gave) |
| "every night, all night" | **Confabulation** | behavioral prediction about a product never used |

## Takeaway captured

Trust the *reactions and language* (comfort, vet trust, orthopedic proof); throw out the *precise numbers* (price, frequency) — the persona will state them confidently but they're invented.
