# Design Three, Interview Three, Separate Signal from Noise

Barkwell is weighing a new line: a "furniture-grade" modern bed built to look like real furniture and fit a stylish home, not to look like a pet product — a premium, design-led departure from its durable-and-practical range. Which new market it fits is an open question. Design three research-seeded personas, interview each about the concept, and document what you'd trust versus what's likely confabulation.

Work in Claude Code. Use:

- [`research-snippets-three-owners.md`](research-snippets-three-owners.md) — research inputs for three distinct owner types.
- [`persona-seed-template.md`](persona-seed-template.md) — the seed structure.
- [`interview-questions.md`](interview-questions.md) — the core question set to run with each persona.
- [`synthesis-example.md`](synthesis-example.md) — a worked example of what a strong synthesis looks like (from a different concept).

## What to produce

A document containing:

- Three seeded persona profiles, each trait traceable to a research input.
- Three interview transcripts or summaries — the core questions run with each persona in a fresh conversation.
- Each persona's reaction to the furniture-grade concept: what resonates, what falls flat, what's missing.
- For each interview, at least one moment of trustworthy signal and one moment of likely confabulation, with the marker that gave it away.
- A short synthesis: which audience the concept actually lands with, where it doesn't, and what you'd carry into a real test.

## Requirements

- Every persona trait must trace to a research input, not invention. If you can't tie a trait to evidence, flag it as an assumption.
- Run each persona in a **fresh conversation** so earlier answers don't anchor later ones.
- Use both confabulation markers to separate signal from noise: a behavioral prediction about something never experienced, and specificity beyond what the seed supports.
- The synthesis must find patterns *across* personas and land a clear read — not just list what each one said. Use the provided example as a model for depth, not as an answer to copy.

## Done when

Your synthesis names which new market the concept fits, states your confidence honestly, and every claim in it traces back to something a persona actually said.
