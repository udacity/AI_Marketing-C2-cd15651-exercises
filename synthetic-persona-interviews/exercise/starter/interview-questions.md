# Core Interview Questions

*Provided template for running a structured pre-flight interview with each synthetic persona. Ask the same core questions of every persona so their answers are comparable. Keep the wording neutral, do not lead the persona toward the answer you want.*

---

## Warm-up (grounds the persona in their real situation)

1. Tell me a bit about your current situation with your dog and how you shop for something like this today.
2. The last time you bought a product like this, what mattered most in your decision?

## Reaction to the concept

3. Here is the concept. What is your honest first reaction?
4. What about it appeals to you, if anything?
5. What feels off, confusing, or missing?

## Objections and barriers

6. What would make you hesitate or decide not to buy this?
7. Is there anything here that feels like it is not for someone like you?

## Purchase and trust

8. What would have to be true for you to actually buy this?
9. Who or what would you trust to tell you this is worth it?

## Close

10. If you had to sum up this concept in one sentence, how would you describe it to a friend?

---

**Note on neutrality:** avoid questions like "You'd love this, right?" or "Isn't the design great?" Leading questions produce agreeable, low-value answers. Ask what they think and what would stop them, not whether they approve.
