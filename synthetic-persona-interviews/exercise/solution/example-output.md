## Synthesis

The *look* appeals across all three (shared signal). Buying divides on **proof and price**: Maya converts if quality is credible; Rick is a hard no until durability/washability are answered; Priya won't pay during the puppy phase. Recurring objection (2 of 3): doubt a "designer" bed is actually well-made.

**Which market fits:** design-conscious urban owners (Maya). **Carry into real testing:** lead with the look, but pair every aesthetic claim with hard durability proof. **Confidence:** medium — 3 synthetic personas are directional; segment sizes need live research.
