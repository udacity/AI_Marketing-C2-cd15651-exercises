# Demo — Vessl Research Request (Sustainability Claims in the Category)

*Author/demo input. The raw request the worked demo delegates to a background agent and then audits. Deliberately a different question from the exercise — the exercise scopes the competitive landscape and market trends — so the demo teaches the method on a fresh case and doesn't pre-solve the exercise.*

**About Vessl:** Vessl makes a smart water bottle that tracks intake and syncs to a companion app. It's writing the sustainability section of its launch messaging, and doesn't want to make a claim the category has already worn out — or one it can't back.

---

**From:** Head of Brand
**To:** You (marketing manager)
**Re:** What sustainability claims is the category already making?

Before I sign off on our packaging and materials copy, I need to know what everyone else in the category is saying — and how much of it is actually backed by something.

- **The claims.** What premium reusable-bottle brands say about materials (recycled content, plastic-free packaging) and about the product's footprint.
- **The backing.** For each claim: is it resting on a third-party certification or audit, or is it the brand's own word?
- **The certifications.** Which schemes keep showing up in this category, and what each one actually certifies.
- **The consumer context.** What research says about whether sustainability claims move purchase decisions here.

Positioning and pricing are handled elsewhere — I only need the sustainability story.

Keep it tight and well-sourced. I'd rather have four claims I can defend than a page I can't. Every claim needs a source I can open.
