# Vessl: Research Request (from the CMO)

*Provided input for the background-agent research exercise. This is the raw request. Your job is to turn it into a well-scoped agent delegation (question, scope, output format, guardrails), run it, and then audit what comes back for source quality.*

---

**From:** CMO
**To:** You (marketing manager)
**Re:** Creator shortlist for the launch

We're going to lead Vessl's launch with creators rather than traditional ads, and I need a vetted shortlist before we start outreach. I don't want to pick names off vibes and follower counts. I want candidates we've actually checked.

Please research a shortlist of real creators we could sponsor, across the spaces our buyer lives in: hydration and wellness, fitness, health and fitness-tech gadget reviewers, sustainability, and everyday lifestyle. For each creator, I want:

- **Who they are and where.** Name, main platform(s), and what they're known for.
- **Audience size and who's in it.** Follower/subscriber counts, and whether their audience actually matches our target, not just how big it is.
- **Engagement, and whether it's real.** Their engagement rate, and any signal about whether the audience is genuine versus inflated or bought.
- **Content style and values.** How they show up, and whether it fits a brand about feeling good and staying hydrated, framed positively.
- **Brand-safety flags.** Anything in their history that would make sponsoring them a risk.
- **Rough cost.** A ballpark on what a sponsorship might run, if you can find it.

Every meaningful claim needs a source I can check. I care more about a small, trustworthy list than a long one, because we're going to put real budget behind whoever we pick.

I need this by end of week to kick off outreach.
